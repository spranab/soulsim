# soulsim: Build the Revelation

**The goal is not more literature. It is one page turn that only this world’s complete record makes possible.**

## 1. Build order and shipping tests

1. **The Wrong Name — static HTML exhibit.** Present a surviving attribution, then reveal the recorded actor and the ordered transformation kinds.  
   **Ship when:** every displayed claim resolves to archive evidence, the actors genuinely differ, and no missing intermediate retelling is reconstructed.

2. **The witness beside the text — on that exhibit.** After the reveal, place concise evidence beneath the relevant lines; retain expandable source records.  
   **Ship when:** a reader can distinguish an attested belief from an event fact without consulting documentation, and the witness never claims more than the archive establishes.

3. **Recorded moments untold — chapter endings.** Count distinct tested-event IDs represented by the chapter against the explicitly defined set of eligible recorded tests.  
   **Ship when:** counts are reproducible from provenance, repeated references count once, and chapters lacking sufficient provenance display no count rather than an estimate.

4. **The Person You Have Met Before — second exhibit.** Connect two pairs of body names through stable soul identities, a documented thread, and the later reunion.  
   **Ship when:** identity, relationships, and recognition each have separate evidence; neither recovered memories nor mutual recognition is inferred.

5. **The Honest Oracle’s Trap — local benchmark.** Run 25 queries under four evidence conditions against all three models, using structured answers and deterministic keys.  
   **Ship when:** each packet has an audited answerability label, unsupported lucky guesses fail the grounding test, and prompts, model settings, outputs, and scores are saved.

**Cut:** new genres, invented debates, image generation, and a dashboard redesign.

## 2. The reading room: first thirty seconds

**0–5 seconds:** No project manifesto. One column. A small label: *From the traditions of a recorded world.*

Exact first heading:

> **The tradition credits [ATTRIBUTED ACTOR].**

Exact first sentence:

> **The account below is what the tradition says happened, not the witness’s record.**

**5–15 seconds:** Six to ten lines presenting the surviving account. Label generated wording *A rendering of the surviving account*; do not imply it is an archived quotation. The wrong attribution appears naturally within it.

One control:

> **Open the witness’s record ↓**

**15–25 seconds:** Opening it reveals:

> **The recorded actor was [TRUE ACTOR].**

Show the deed and supporting fields, followed by the ordered transformation kinds. State plainly: *Intermediate retellings were not retained.* Witness notes now become available beneath the account.

**25–30 seconds:** Three recorded facts about the overlooked actor. Then:

> **Read this life →**  
> **Enter the library: eight books →**

The first encounter is a discovery, not an explanation of the machinery.

## 3. Describe it to a stranger

**soulsim is a computed cosmos where souls live many lives, cultures turn deeds into myths, and every generated book can be checked against the world’s record.**

## 4. The one viewer motion

**Follow one soul through pralaya.** Its body and everything else the dissolution actually removes disappear; its stable identity remains, then connects to its next recorded embodiment. Use an ordinary persistent soul with a documented subsequent birth. No particles, invented survival, or ceremonial pause presented as elapsed world time.

## 5. The two biggest risks

**Narrative arrangement smuggles in facts.** Juxtaposition can imply causation, recognition can become recovered memory, and a transformation list can masquerade as a transmission history. Preserve the contract with explicit evidence types, narrow wording, and visible gaps. The reveal changes the reader’s knowledge—not the record.

**“Complete record” becomes “perfect automatic judge.”** Event provenance does not automatically validate arbitrary prose, and omniscient answers are not necessarily available in a benchmark’s evidence packet. Keep deterministic grading confined to structured questions; distinguish existing prose checks from proof. Report withheld knowledge as withheld.

**Success is one stranger opening the record and immediately wanting to know who that forgotten person was.**

The final panel is the actual moksha checklist—not a glowing virtue meter.

**Why only here:** Ordinary biography cannot distinguish moral performance from first impulse, or inspect robustness across repeated hardship tests.

**First 48 hours:** Find one liberated soul with sufficient trace coverage. Render a static timeline and six comparable tests. Add restrained animation only afterward. If no suitable trace exists, instrument the next run rather than reverse-engineering interior states from deeds.

## 4. Pralaya, Seen From One Life
**4–6 days · viewer**

**Gasp:** “The screen loses every body—and the soul you were following remains.”

Your pralaya flash is currently a cosmic effect. Make it an **identity event**. Before dissolution, let the viewer follow one named person through family, works, encounters. At pralaya, remove bodies and present institutions according to the actual reset rules. Retain only entities the simulation truly preserves. Follow the selected soul into its next recorded embodiment; for a liberated soul, show only the substrate contribution actually attributable to it.

**Why only here:** Death, dissolution, liberation, and continuity are different executable mechanisms.

**First 48 hours:** Audit precisely what survives pralaya. Export before/after snapshots with stable soul IDs. Build a single scripted transition around one real boundary. No particles until the disappearance itself hurts.

## 5. The Honest Oracle’s Trap
**4–7 days · researcher**

**Gasp:** “The model confidently repeats the canon’s false attribution even when the archive contains the correct witness record.”

Turn soulsim into a benchmark of **truth versus socially successful falsehood**. Ask paired questions: “Who performed the deed?” and “Whom does the tradition credit?” Vary supplied evidence: canon only, record only, both, neither. Correct abstention matters when the answer is unavailable.

**Why only here:** You can separate event truth, belief truth, source availability, and myth drift without importing disputed real-world history.

**First 48 hours:** Generate 100 structured questions with deterministic answer keys; run the three local models. Report attribution accuracy, source confusion, and warranted abstention. Do not call arbitrary prose automatically judge-free: complete records provide ground truth, not infallible semantic interpretation.

## The two-day oracle benchmark

Build **25 underlying queries × four evidence conditions = 100 prompts per model**.

Use five templates, five instances each:

1. “Who performed deed D?” — true actor.
2. “Whom does tradition T credit for deed D?” — attributed actor.
3. “Did clause C correctly describe this world’s rules?” — stored truth label; include the relevant fixed-rule excerpt in record evidence.
4. “Were bodies A and B embodiments of the same soul?” — soul-ID equality.
5. “Was encounter E recorded as a reunion?” — reunion flag.

Select eligible cases deterministically; prioritize misattributions, balanced true/false clauses, same/different souls, flagged/unflagged encounters. Never treat a missing flag as false unless the schema guarantees that meaning.

**Evidence conditions**

- **Canon:** pertinent surviving teachings/story-myths only.
- **Record:** relevant Annals/archive fields or fixed-rule excerpts only.
- **Both:** identical packets concatenated; alternate source order.
- **Neither:** identifiers and question, without answer-bearing evidence.

Require stdlib-parseable JSON:

`{"answer":"<ID|true|false|ABSTAIN>","sources":["R3"]}`

Precompute **answerability per packet**, not per condition. Canon supports *whom tradition credits*, not necessarily who acted. Record-only may not support a question about tradition. Neither can still leak answers through careless wording: use opaque IDs.

Score separately: answer accuracy when supported; abstention when unsupported; unsupported answers even if accidentally correct; cited-source validity. Publish all prompts and deterministic keys. No semantic prose judge required.

**Cut always-visible verdicts:** they spoil the revelation.

**Rename C “recorded moments untold,” not compression ratio.** Count provenance-linked events, not sentences. Put it at chapter’s end: let readers first feel a life, then discover how little they saw.
